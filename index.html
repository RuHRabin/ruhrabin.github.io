About

A short version of who I am and what I'm chasing right now.

I'm Rabin — a lifelong learner driven by curiosity, working through complex problems for the sake of understanding them, not just solving them.

Right now I'm focused on machine learning and simulation, and I spend a lot of time in Linux, building small command-line tools that scratch a very specific itch — like stripping hidden metadata out of images and video before they ever leave my machine.

I keep almost everything I build in public. Half-finished experiments included.

What I'm working on

Three threads I keep pulling on, in no particular order.

Machine learning & simulation
