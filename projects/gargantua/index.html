Gargantua

Black-hole flight game with real-time lensing.

Fly a small ship around a black hole. A fragment shader ray-marches light through Schwarzschild-style curvature, so the lensing is computed per pixel; the clock runs on time dilation; the score is generated live with Web Audio. Three.js, React, Vite.

Fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Interstellar.
